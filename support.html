Frequently Asked

Where is my data stored?
In your own iCloud account. Recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and pantry items sync privately across your devices via iCloud.

Do I need an AI subscription?
No. Core features work without any AI. Optionally, you can connect your own Claude or OpenAI API key (or use on-device Apple Intelligence) for menu suggestions, recipe import, and cooking plans.

How do I share with family?
Open the People tab and invite family members — they'll see your shared recipes and meal plans in their Family Kitchen, and can pick dishes to cook together. Friends can be added by search, invite link, or scanning your profile QR code.

Will my timers ring if my phone is on silent?
Yes. Hachanah uses alarms for cooking timers, so they ring on silent and during a Focus. Allow alarms when the app asks; if you said no, turn them on in Settings → Hachanah.

How do I cook with my family or a guest?
Start cooking a dish from a family meal and everyone in your family gets a prompt to join and pick a dish. To bring in someone outside the family, open the Kitchen Board, tap the ••• menu, and choose Invite a Guest to show a QR code or link.

Can I use my iPad as a second screen?
Yes. When you cook on your iPhone, your iPad (signed in to the same iCloud account and nearby) offers to show the steps or your prep checklist.

How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes local data; to remove synced data, delete Hachanah's data under Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. To remove a public profile or published collections, unpublish them in the app or contact us.
