Hachanah Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Hachanah is a kosher meal-planning app. We built it to keep your data yours: there are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.

What data the app uses

  • Your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, and pantry are stored on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database). We never see this data — it is protected by your Apple ID and accessible only to you and the family members you explicitly share with.
  • Name and email address are used to create your optional public profile and to let friends find you and send you recipes. Your profile may also include an optional username, bio, photo, and phone number — all provided by you, all optional.
  • Location (if you grant permission) is used only to calculate local candle-lighting and havdalah times. It is not stored on our servers and is never linked to your identity.

Social features

  • If you create a profile, your display name, username, and any bio/photo you add are visible to other Hachanah users who search for you. A privacy toggle lets you hide your profile from search — hidden profiles can only be found by someone who already knows your exact email address, phone number, or username.
  • Recipes, collections, or meal plans you explicitly share are visible to the recipients you choose. Collections you publish to Discover are publicly visible until you unpublish them.

Optional AI features

If you connect your own Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI API key, recipe text and ingredient lists are sent to that provider to power features like recipe import and menu suggestions — under your own account and that provider's privacy policy. If you use on-device Apple Intelligence instead, nothing leaves your device. AI features are off until you configure them, and your API keys are stored in the device Keychain.

Third-party services

The app fetches public data (such as zmanim/holiday dates and product/recipe information) from services like Hebcal and Open Food Facts. These requests do not include your identity.

Data retention & deletion

Your private data lives in your iCloud account and can be deleted at any time by removing Hachanah's data in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or by deleting content in the app. Public content (profile, published collections, shared recipes) can be removed in the app, or contact us and we will remove it.

Children

Hachanah is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
